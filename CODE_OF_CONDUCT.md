# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant]. All maintainers,
contributors, and users must follow it in all project spaces: issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other place where you represent the project.

## Our standards

Examples of good behavior:

- Show empathy and kindness to other people.
- Respect different opinions, points of view, and experiences.
- Give and accept helpful feedback in a polite way.
- Accept responsibility for your mistakes, apologize to the people they affect, and learn from
  them.
- Do what is best for the community, not only for yourself.

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexual language or images, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing private information of other people, such as a physical or email address, without
  their permission.
- Other behavior that is not appropriate in a professional setting.

## Report a problem

Report behavior that is not acceptable to the project maintainers at
**[CONTACT EMAIL]**. The maintainers review all reports quickly and fairly, and keep the
identity of the reporter private.

## Enforcement

The maintainers can remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. They use the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines][enforcement] (correction, warning, temporary ban,
permanent ban) to decide the result.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
[enforcement]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines
